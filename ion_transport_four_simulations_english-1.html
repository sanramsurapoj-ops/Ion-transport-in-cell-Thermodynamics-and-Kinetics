Eᵢ is calculated from the Nernst equation, Eᵢ = (RT/zF) ln(aₒᵤₜ/aᵢₙ), using concentrations as an ideal-solution approximation. Current uses the ohmic model I = g(Vₘ − Eᵢ); nS × mV = pA.
